# Theme & palettes

Two palette families for two jobs: qualitative palettes for *categories*
(they recycle, with a warning), sequential ramps for *intensity* (they
interpolate). Painting four coverage tracks from a 20-colour qualitative
palette is the mistake the split exists to prevent.
