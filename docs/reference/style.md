# Theme & palettes

Three colour vocabularies for three jobs: `FEATURE_COLOURS` names the
*roles* in a gene-model figure and feeds every geom's default, qualitative
palettes are for *categories* (they recycle, with a warning), sequential
ramps for *intensity* (they interpolate). Painting four coverage tracks
from a 20-colour qualitative palette is the mistake the split exists to
prevent.
